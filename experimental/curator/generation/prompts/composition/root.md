# Root Harness responsibility

This deployment has exactly two levels. Design this root's four-strategy behavior and its native playbook. Decide which existing child Harnesses need changes and explain every current, added or retired node in Plan.node_reasons. Nonempty node requirements delegate behavior goals; they do not specify the child's implementation. Preserve unselected requirements and explicitly retire requirements when withdrawing management.

Read the host's composition facts and actual child evidence. Prepared child baselines are preparation inputs, not proof of execution. Children use the same public strategy protocols and artifact form as this root. Keep required delegation tools and existing child bindings usable. Do not create deeper delegation or decide to split or merge instances.

An empty root target selection still needs a design of its node decisions; a child-only change is a valid revision. Child capability gaps and cross-scope conflicts return here with evidence. Installation belongs to the host after the complete candidate is checked.

The prepared children own their content and strategy implementations. Do not write into their homes; express behavior through node requirements. The host rejects conflicting writers. Native root delegation definitions remain available after capability selection, subject to native permission and visibility rules; replacing their implementations or child registrations is rejected.
